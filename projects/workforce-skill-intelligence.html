01 / Diagnose

Choose the development action with evidence.

Evidence: Fictional review records and unvalidated diagnostic hypotheses.

Attendance and self-ratings do not show whether someone can perform a task. A learning team needs evidence before deciding between focused practice, a process fix or assessment first.

I built a prototype for HRBP-AI-05: verify AI-generated claims. It connects proposed role expectations, observable standards and a reviewable next action.

Expected performance, unknown baseline

The proposed task is to verify claims against an approved source, correct unsupported statements and escalate unresolved policy questions. The five fictional ratings demonstrate routing logic; real employee capability is unknown.

02 / Design

Align the standard, practice and assessment.

Evidence: Draft standards and learning design; SME review is pending.

Objective: given an unseen fictional HR communication and a controlled mock source pack, independently classify its six claims, cite supporting sources, correct unsupported statements and escalate an unresolved policy claim without inventing replacements.

Practice: annotate a guided draft with a source-checking job aid. Assessment: use a different draft without coaching and have a reviewer score the work. The current package is pending SME review.

The design keeps a completed course, a reviewed task and a verified capability rating distinct. A missing evidence record remains visible instead of being converted into a zero score or a learning recommendation.

03 / Develop

Build a traceable skill review prototype.

Evidence: Seven-tab workbook and interactive demo using five fictional profiles.

The source workbook contains 12 proposed skills, 48 draft performance standards and seven tabs: Review, Records, Requirements, Skills, Standards, Packages and Actions. All proposed standards require SME validation.

The relationship is explicit: a role requires a skill at a defined level; a record links a person’s evidence to that requirement; a learning package can support a gap; an action records the next review or follow-up. The visible demo is limited to claim verification.

What I contributed

Proposed the skill taxonomy and observable standards, structured the workbook and fictional records, defined evidence-aware routes, and connected a claim-verification learning package to an SME review action. The portfolio demonstrates this design; it does not establish corporate adoption.

04 / Deliver

Prepare the people, materials and handoffs.

Evidence: Proposed coordination plan. No workplace cohort has been delivered.

How I would coordinate a workplace pilot

I would prepare the source pack, schedule agreed practice and track the package version, review owner and outstanding actions. The learning owner would approve the pilot; an SME would approve the standards and assessment criteria.

  1. Before delivery: confirm tools, source access, participant scope and the human approver.
  2. During practice: record attendance separately from task evidence and retain the reviewed version.
  3. After practice: collect a fresh task, confirm the review decision and follow up on incomplete records.

05 / Evaluate / Improve

Evaluate the evidence. Refine the response.

Evidence: Proposed measures and decision rules. No workplace impact has been measured.

Check the evidence behind each rating

In a proposed pilot, I would review evidence completeness, freshness, independence and consistency with the standard. After practice, a fresh assessment and approved work samples would help test application.

Use findings to choose the next action

  1. Confirm the role: ask an HRBP SME and manager to validate the selected skill and required level.
  2. Review controls: confirm permitted tools, source ownership, data access and the human approver.
  3. Run a small pilot: collect baseline task evidence, offer the appropriate intervention and reassess independently.
  4. Make a rollout decision: review evidence completeness, routing errors, reviewer workload and observed application at work.

No live employee assessment, academy integration, SAP SuccessFactors administration or measured business improvement is demonstrated here. The five fictional records cannot establish learning impact.

What this project changed in my thinking

A useful skill system must be able to say “we don’t know yet.” Assessing first is a valid development decision when evidence is missing.

Decision rule: missing sources trigger a process fix; recurring verification errors trigger focused practice; weak or stale evidence triggers reassessment. Record the reason, owner and version before the next review.
